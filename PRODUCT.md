# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Israelis looking for a task manager (confirmed). They currently use Todoist, Things, or a
notes app, and they read and write Hebrew all day. The recurring frustration the product
answers is a Hebrew interface that was translated from English rather than built in Hebrew:
mirrored-but-wrong layouts, English quick-add syntax, a Gregorian-only calendar, and dates
that read left-to-right.

Secondary, unconfirmed: keyboard-driven power users. Not a stated target; do not build the
page around them.

## Product Purpose

סדר is a personal task manager written in Hebrew, right-to-left from the ground up. It exists
so an Israeli can capture, plan and finish work without translating their own thinking.
Success is a visitor signing up for a free account and typing their first task in Hebrew.

## Positioning

Confirmed by the user as the one-second proof: **real Hebrew, not a translation.**

Concretely, and not truthfully copyable by a translated competitor:

- The quick-add composer parses Hebrew as you type — `לסיים מצגת מחר בשעה 14:30 #עבודה !1`
  saves a task titled exactly `לסיים מצגת`, scheduled tomorrow 14:30, in project עבודה,
  priority 1. Everything understood is stripped from the title and shown as chips first.
- Every calendar cell carries both calendars: the Gregorian day in tabular figures and the
  Hebrew day in gematria. The Hebrew month is named once, on ראש חודש.
- Sunday-first grid running right-to-left, with Friday and Saturday shaded as the Israeli
  weekend.
- A second, independent axis borrowed from Things: **schedule** (when you will work on it) is
  separate from **deadline** (when it must be ready). A task with both appears twice on the
  calendar on purpose.

## Operating Context

Views: תיבה נכנסת, היום, בקרוב, בכל עת, מתישהו, יומן, plus projects, labels and a calendar
(month and week). Two independent axes: **where** (filed into a project, or Inbox) and **when**
(scheduled with a date / בכל עת / מתישהו), with an optional deadline on top.

Repeating tasks complete per-occurrence: the done copy lands in the Logbook and the live task
rolls to its next date, deadline offset preserved. `Ctrl/⌘ K` is search plus jump-to-anything.
Appearance is two independent axes: brightness (system/light/dark) and one of seven accent
hues.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 15 App Router, React 19, Tailwind v4, Prisma + SQLite, argon2 sessions.
- `<html lang="he" dir="rtl">` at the root; RTL is the only direction the product ships.
- Three type roles already fixed: Frank Ruhl Libre (display / date lockup / view titles),
  Assistant (interface), Inter (numerals only, tabular, LTR islands).
- Colour is a token system in `src/app/globals.css` using `light-dark()`; components never
  reference a raw colour. Amber means time pressure and red means priority 1 — neither may be
  reused for anything else.
- Free account, email + password, with password reset. No pricing, no plans, no team features.
- Production: lawebs.co.il/seder.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **סדר**. Wordmark is the word itself, set in Frank Ruhl Libre.
- Voice: plain modern Hebrew, dugri, no marketing padding, no hype adjectives, no exclamation
  marks. The product's own words are the interface's words (היום, בקרוב, מתישהו, תיבה נכנסת).
- Gender-neutral phrasing by rewording (`אפשר`, `יש ל…`), not slash notation.
- The existing design system is inherited, not replaced.

## Evidence on Hand

- README.md — full, accurate feature truth.
- `screenshots/` — captured screenshots of every view, generated by `node scripts/shots.mjs`.
- A seeded demo account (`demo@seder.app`) exists locally, for development only.

**Absent, and must not be fabricated:** user counts, customer logos, testimonials, reviews,
press, ratings, uptime figures, funding, awards, pricing tiers, mobile apps, integrations,
an API, offline sync, or collaboration.

## Product Principles

1. Hebrew is the source language, not a locale. Anything that reads as translated is a bug.
2. Two axes stay separate — where a task is filed and when it is planned — and a deadline is
   neither of them.
3. Quiet by default, with exactly one delightful moment: completing a task.
4. The keyboard is a first-class input, never the only one.
5. Claim nothing the product cannot demonstrate on screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body text floor is 16px; Hebrew is never letter-spaced. All text tokens clear WCAG AA on every
surface in both themes, audited by `node scripts/contrast-check.mjs`. One visible focus
treatment everywhere. `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured. Israeli standard IS 5568 (WCAG 2.0
AA) is the relevant legal bar for a public Israeli site.
